# Research report checklist

- [ ] The executive summary answers the question directly.
- [ ] Every sub-question has a section with evidence or an explicit "insufficient evidence".
- [ ] Every factual claim has a citation; every citation appears in References.
- [ ] Contested claims show both sides and why they differ.
- [ ] Numbers carry units, dates and sources.
- [ ] Limitations section is honest and specific.
- [ ] No source is cited that was not actually read in this run.
