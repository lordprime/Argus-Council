# Executive summary

Structure (aim for under 300 words):

1. **Bottom line** — one or two sentences that answer the question or recommend a decision.
2. **Why** — three bullets of the strongest evidence, each with a number or citation.
3. **Options** — up to three, each with cost/benefit in one line.
4. **Risks** — the two that would change the recommendation.
5. **Next steps** — owner-ready actions.

No jargon without definition. No hedging beyond what the evidence requires.
